# Aqina Craft-Focused 30s English Video Design

## Style Prompt

Premium natural-industrial craft video for a square Facebook ad. The focus is Aqina's own Pineapple Enzyme Chickens, controlled Malaysian closed-house raising, MD2 pineapple enzyme nutrition, double-boil steaming, slow drip extraction, and strict batch checks. The video should feel clean, warm, natural, and trustworthy, not laboratory-made or foreign-farm stock.

## Colors

- Deep evergreen background: `#091a14`
- Forest panel: `#112b22`
- Warm ivory text: `#f8f5ec`
- Aqina heritage gold: `#d4af37`
- Bright pineapple gold: `#ffb800`
- Soft rice gold surface: `#f8ecd5`
- Warm brown copy: `#4d351f`

## Typography

- Display: `Cormorant Garamond`, serif
- Body and captions: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Noto Sans SC`, sans-serif

## Motion

- Calm premium motion with light-leak wipes between scenes.
- Closed house, feed flow, steam, and drip extraction should be schematic, warm, and deterministic.
- Use real product, pineapple, and golden essence assets where they strengthen trust.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use foreign outdoor farm/person imagery.
- Do not make the production look like a laboratory.
- Do not claim medical treatment, cure, recovery, or disease benefits.
- Do not use jump cuts between scenes.
- Do not overcrowd the 1080 square canvas.
