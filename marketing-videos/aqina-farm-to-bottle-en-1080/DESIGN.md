# Aqina Farm-to-Bottle English Video Design

## Style Prompt

Premium wellness documentary for a square Facebook video ad in English. The visual tone is traceable, warm, clean, and agricultural-tech, pairing real farm/product imagery with restrained editorial motion. The video should feel trustworthy and appetizing, not medical, clinical, or overly promotional.

## Colors

- Deep evergreen background: `#091a14`
- Forest panel: `#112b22`
- Warm ivory text: `#f8f5ec`
- Aqina heritage gold: `#d4af37`
- Bright pineapple gold: `#ffb800`
- Soft rice gold surface: `#f8ecd5`
- Warm brown copy: `#6f5a43`

## Typography

- Display: `Cormorant Garamond`, fallback `Songti SC`, serif
- Body and captions: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, fallback `PingFang SC`, `Noto Sans SC`, sans-serif

## Motion

- Calm premium transitions: light-leak wipe and soft blur crossfade.
- Entrances use gentle y/scale/opacity movement with `power3.out`, `expo.out`, and `sine.out`.
- Decorative particle and glow motion must be finite and deterministic.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use medical treatment, recovery, cure, or disease claims.
- Do not address the viewer's health condition directly.
- Do not use loud neon, purple-blue gradients, or stock-looking empty placeholders.
- Do not use jump cuts between scenes.
- Do not crowd the square canvas with long bilingual text.
