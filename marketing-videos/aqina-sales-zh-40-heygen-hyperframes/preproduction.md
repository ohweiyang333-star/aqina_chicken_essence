## Production Route

- Level: Level 2
- Spoken language: Chinese Mandarin
- HeyGen route: private photo avatar A-roll
- Recommended avatar: `947d57c73cfa4bc892fa615c0766a159` / `Warm-Faced Chinese Woman in Cream Blouse, Sunlit Home`
- Voice candidate: HeyGen multilingual female friendly voice, prefer `Melissa - Friendly` or equivalent Mandarin-compatible voice

## Product And Claims Guardrails

- Use `纯鸡精` in all Chinese customer-facing text.
- Do not use disease, recovery, fertility, pregnancy, or guaranteed health claims.
- Position as daily nourishment and easy routine.
- CTA should invite consultation instead of hard-selling a price too early.

## HeyGen Generation Notes

Use a clean A-roll only:

- No burned-in captions from HeyGen.
- No HeyGen b-roll.
- No Product Placement unless the standard avatar route fails.
- Natural short-ad speed around 1.25x if the selected voice supports it.
- Motion prompt: calm, friendly, direct-to-camera presenter, light hand gestures, natural smile.

## QA Criteria

- Duration lands around 35-40 seconds after HyperFrames packaging.
- Product name remains Aqina 纯鸡精.
- Product close-ups do not block face or captions.
- Subtitle blocks are readable on mobile feed.
- CTA uses landing page, WhatsApp, and Facebook Messenger labels.
- Final MP4 is Facebook-compatible and visually checked before delivery.
